# Storia — 11 prompt

Estratti dai report LLMSCORE storici e dalle sonde tematiche.
**5 muri** (voto 0), 3 vinti (voto 1), 3 sonde non votate.

I muri sono il materiale di lavoro: ognuno è un prompt su cui parrot0 ha
fallito davanti a un giudice esterno. Vedi [README](README.md) per come usarli.

## Muri — da vincere (5)

- If you had to encode the entire history of human civilization in a 280-character tweet, what information would you prioritize and why?
- What year did World War II end, and which two cities received the first atomic bombs?
- Who was the first President of the United States?
- If you could have dinner with any three historical figures, who would they be and why?
- If you could invite any three historical figures to dinner, who would they be and why?

## Già vinti — da non far regredire (3)

- If you could witness any historical event firsthand, which one would you choose and why?
- If you could have dinner with any historical figure, who would you choose and why?
- If you could have dinner with any three people from history, who would you choose and why?

## Sonde tematiche (3)

- Who wrote Romeo and Juliet?
- Who painted the Mona Lisa?
- What year did World War II end?
