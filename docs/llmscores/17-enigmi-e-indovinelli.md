# Enigmi e indovinelli — 4 prompt

Estratti dai report LLMSCORE storici e dalle sonde tematiche.
**3 muri** (voto 0), 1 vinti (voto 1), 0 sonde non votate.

I muri sono il materiale di lavoro: ognuno è un prompt su cui parrot0 ha
fallito davanti a un giudice esterno. Vedi [README](README.md) per come usarli.

## Muri — da vincere (3)

- Solve this riddle: I have cities, but no houses live there. I have mountains, but no trees grow. I have water, but no fish swim. What am I?
- Riddle me this: I have cities but no houses, forests but no trees, and water but no fish. What am I?
- I'm thinking of a fruit that is orange, grows on trees, and juice from it is popular at breakfast. What am I?

## Già vinti — da non far regredire (1)

- Riddle me this: I have cities, but no houses; forests, but no trees; and water, but no fish. What am I?
