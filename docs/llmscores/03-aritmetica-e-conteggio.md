# Aritmetica e conteggio — 67 prompt

Estratti dai report LLMSCORE storici e dalle sonde tematiche.
**25 muri** (voto 0), 33 vinti (voto 1), 9 sonde non votate.

I muri sono il materiale di lavoro: ognuno è un prompt su cui parrot0 ha
fallito davanti a un giudice esterno. Vedi [README](README.md) per come usarli.

## Muri — da vincere (25)

- If a mathematician discovers a new prime number that can be expressed as the sum of 1,000 consecutive integers, how would they verify this property?
- If a store sells 3 apples for $2, how much would you pay for 15 apples, and how much change would you get from a $20 bill?
- How many times does the digit 7 appear between 1 and 100?
- If a store sells 8 apples for $2 and 12 oranges for $3, how much would you pay for 4 apples and 6 oranges?
- If you arrange the letters in "FLUFFY NOSE" as a single string, how many valid English words can you find that are exactly 4 letters long?
- How many prime numbers are there between 1 and 100?
- If you have a rectangular garden that is 12 meters long and 8 meters wide, and you want to place square tiles that are 2 meters on each side, how many tiles will you need to cover the entire garden?
- A baker puts 3 cookies on each plate. If she has 7 plates and fills them all, then puts the remaining cookies in a jar, how many cookies were in the jar if she started with 30 cookies?
- Count backward from 100 by 7s, writing down each number. When you reach a number divisible by 5, say "stop" instead of the number.
- If a store sells 47 oranges in the morning and 3 times as many in the afternoon, how many oranges did it sell in total that day?
- A farmer has 17 sheep, sells all but 9, then buys 12 more. How many sheep does he have now?
- How many letters are in the word "strawberry"?
- A farmer has 20 animals—chickens and rabbits. Together they have 56 legs. How many chickens does the farmer have?
- If a train travels 120 miles in 2 hours, and another train travels 180 miles in 3 hours, how much faster is the second train going per hour?
- If you have 3 apples and I give you 4 more, then you eat 2, how many apples do you have left?
- Count backward from 20 to 1, but skip any number that ends in 5.
- A baker uses 3 eggs to make a cake. If she wants to make 4 cakes and she has 10 eggs, how many more eggs does she need to buy?
- If a train travels 60 miles per hour for 2 hours, and then stops for 30 minutes, how many total hours has the journey taken so far?
- If you have 3 apples and you give 1 to a friend and then buy 2 more at the store, how many apples do you have?
- A bat and ball cost $1.10 total. The bat costs $1 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
- A farmer has 20 chickens and cows combined. If there are 56 total legs, how many of each animal does the farmer have?
- Count backward from 20 to 1, but say only the odd numbers.
- How many fingers do you have on one hand?
- That's okay — let's try something different. If you have 3 apples and I give you 2 more, then eat 1, how many apples do you have?
- You started with 3 apples, I gave you 2 more (that's 5 total), then you ate 1, leaving you with **4 apples**. You answered 6, which would only be right if you didn't eat one. Want to try another?
