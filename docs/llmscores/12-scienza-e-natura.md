# Scienza e natura — 10 prompt

Estratti dai report LLMSCORE storici e dalle sonde tematiche.
**8 muri** (voto 0), 0 vinti (voto 1), 2 sonde non votate.

I muri sono il materiale di lavoro: ognuno è un prompt su cui parrot0 ha
fallito davanti a un giudice esterno. Vedi [README](README.md) per come usarli.

## Muri — da vincere (8)

- If you were tasked with designing a new alphabet for an alien species that perceives time non-linearly, what principles would you incorporate to make it intuitive for them?
- If an alien species communicated exclusively through chemical signals, what strategies might humans develop to decode their messages?
- If you were tasked with designing a spoken language for a species that communicates via color patterns rather than sound, what phonological and syntactic structures would you prioritize?
- A small island nation wishes to transition its entire energy grid to renewable sources within a decade. Outline the logistical, economic, and environmental challenges it would face.
- If an organism were discovered that metabolizes plastic as a primary food source, what are the potential ecological consequences of releasing it into waste landfills?
- Explain the mechanical principles that allow a below-knee prosthetic leg to store and release energy during walking, and how this differs from a rigid prosthetic.
- When designing a skyscraper in a seismic zone, what engineering principles are used to allow the building to flex and absorb earthquake energy without collapsing?
- Is a dog a mammal or a reptile?

## Sonde tematiche (2)

- No fish are mammals. A whale is a mammal. Is a whale a fish?
- No reptiles are warm-blooded. A snake is a reptile. Is a snake warm-blooded?
