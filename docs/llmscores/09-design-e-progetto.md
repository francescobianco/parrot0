Estratti dai report LLMSCORE storici e dalle sonde tematiche.

I muri sono il materiale di lavoro: ognuno è un prompt su cui parrot0 ha

## Muri — da vincere (14)

- Describe a plausible scenario where renewable energy sources become the dominant power grid in a city within a decade.
- Propose a method for measuring the cultural impact of a viral meme on a specific demographic.
- Design a board game that can be played with a standard deck of playing cards, describing its objective, setup, and turn structure.
- If a library contained only one copy of every book ever written, how would you design a retrieval system that could satisfy a million simultaneous requests without digital technology?
- How would you design an experiment to test whether a dog can understand the concept of 'tomorrow' as opposed to just immediate reward?
- If a city were built entirely underground with no natural light, how might its architecture evolve to create a sense of 'day' and 'night' for its inhabitants?
- How would you design a zero‑gravity cooking experiment that tests the diffusion of flavors in a sealed container aboard the International Space Station?
- Propose a method for teaching a child to understand the concept of recursion using only everyday objects.
- If a fictional planet has a day that lasts exactly 100 Earth hours, how would you design a timekeeping system that keeps its calendar aligned with its orbit?
- Propose a framework for evaluating the credibility of a scientific claim in a post‑truth era, emphasizing the role of peer review and open data.
- Design a cooperative board game where players work together to build a space colony while dealing with random equipment failures and resource scarcity.
- If you were to design a password that is both highly secure and easy for a human to remember, what principles from cognitive psychology would you apply?
- Design a simple protocol for transmitting text messages between two submarines submerged at a depth of 100 meters, considering the physical constraints of underwater communication.
- How did Esperanto and Klingon differ in their design goals and linguistic structures, and what does this reveal about the challenges of creating a universal language?
