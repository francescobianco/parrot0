# Spiegazione causale — 24 prompt

Estratti dai report LLMSCORE storici e dalle sonde tematiche.
**13 muri** (voto 0), 2 vinti (voto 1), 9 sonde non votate.

I muri sono il materiale di lavoro: ognuno è un prompt su cui parrot0 ha
fallito davanti a un giudice esterno. Vedi [README](README.md) per come usarli.

## Muri — da vincere (13)

- Explain how the concept of 'loss aversion' can be applied to persuade someone to adopt a healthier lifestyle.
- Explain why the human brain is susceptible to optical illusions and provide an example that illustrates this susceptibility.
- What is the minimum number of cuts required to separate a Möbius strip into two distinct pieces, and can you explain why?
- Explain how the principle of Occam’s Razor could be applied to choose between two competing psychological theories of memory.
- A chef adds a pinch of salt to a dessert that is primarily sugar, and the perceived sweetness increases. Explain the sensory mechanism behind this effect.
- During a solar eclipse, why does the sky not become completely dark even when the Moon fully covers the Sun, and what role does the Sun's corona play?
- In quantum entanglement, if two particles become entangled and then one is measured, the other's state is instantly determined. Does this imply information can travel faster than light, and why do most physicists say no?
- Why do certain musical chords sound tense and require resolution, and how does this tension‑release cycle relate to the auditory system's predictions?
- Why do violin family instruments produce a richer harmonic spectrum than a flute, and how does this affect the perceived timbre of each?
- Describe the atmospheric conditions required for the formation of cumulonimbus clouds, and explain how changes in humidity and temperature gradients affect their development.
- After making a series of decisions throughout the day, why do people tend to make riskier choices in the evening, and what does this suggest about the nature of willpower?
- Tell me a complete sentence that starts with the word "Because" and ends with the word "until.
- Complete this sentence: "The sky is blue because...

## Già vinti — da non far regredire (2)

- Explain why the sky appears blue during the day.
- What's your favorite type of weather and why do you enjoy it?

- Why do we get hiccups?

- Why do onions make you cry?
