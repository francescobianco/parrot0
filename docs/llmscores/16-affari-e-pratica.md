## Muri — da vincere (4)

- What strategies would you suggest for a small business to increase its online presence without relying on social media platforms?
- A company wants to use CRISPR to eradicate a vector‑borne disease by releasing genetically modified mosquitoes into the wild. What ecological risk‑assessment steps should they undertake?
- In a sealed economy with limited resources, if the government implements price controls on essential goods, what are the likely short-term and long-term economic consequences?
- A store sells a watch for $30. The owner gives a $5 discount, then a customer uses a $10 coupon. What did the watch actually cost?
