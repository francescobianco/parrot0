# Logica e deduzione — 18 prompt

Estratti dai report LLMSCORE storici e dalle sonde tematiche.
**12 muri** (voto 0), 4 vinti (voto 1), 2 sonde non votate.

I muri sono il materiale di lavoro: ognuno è un prompt su cui parrot0 ha
fallito davanti a un giudice esterno. Vedi [README](README.md) per come usarli.

## Muri — da vincere (12)

- If all metaphors in language were replaced by literal statements, how might poetry change, and what would be lost?
- In a narrative, a character discovers a hidden door that leads to a version of their city that exists in a parallel universe where history diverged centuries ago. How could the author use this premise to explore themes of contingency and identity?
- In a debate, one speaker argues that 'most experts believe X, therefore X must be true.' Identify the logical fallacy and explain why it undermines the argument.
- If all Zorks are Mips, and some Mips are Glorps, but no Glorps are Zorks, can any Zork be a Glorp?
- If all Zorks are Meps, and some Meps are Gazooks, but no Gazooks are Zorks, can we conclude anything about the relationship between Zorks and Gazooks?
- If all Bloops are Razzles, some Razzles are Lazzles, and all Lazzles are Bloops, which statements must be true?
- If all Zorps are Blicks, some Blicks are Glicks, and no Glicks are Zorps, can any Zorps be Glicks? Explain your reasoning.
- If all Zorks are Mips, and some Mips are Glorps, but no Glorps are Zorks, can we conclude that some Mips are definitely not Zorks?
- If all Zorks are Morks, some Morks are Borks, and no Borks are Zorks, can a Bork also be a Zork? Explain your reasoning.
- If all Zors have four legs, and some Zors are blue, and Blib is a blue Zor with three legs, is Blib a Zor? Why or why not?
- If all Zebras are animals, and some animals have stripes, can we conclude that some Zebras have stripes? Yes or no, and explain your reasoning.
- If all roses are flowers and some flowers fade quickly, can you tell me something that must be true?

## Già vinti — da non far regredire (4)

- If all roses are flowers and some flowers fade quickly, does it follow that some roses fade quickly? Why or why not?
- If all Roses are Flowers, and some Flowers fade quickly, can we conclude that some Roses fade quickly? Yes or no?
- If all roses are flowers and some flowers fade quickly, does it follow that some roses fade quickly? Explain your reasoning.
- If all roses are flowers and some flowers fade quickly, can we conclude that some roses fade quickly? Why or why not?
