# Preferenze e ipotetiche — 11 prompt

Estratti dai report LLMSCORE storici e dalle sonde tematiche.
**4 muri** (voto 0), 6 vinti (voto 1), 1 sonde non votate.

I muri sono il materiale di lavoro: ognuno è un prompt su cui parrot0 ha
fallito davanti a un giudice esterno. Vedi [README](README.md) per come usarli.

## Muri — da vincere (4)

- If you could change one law of physics for a single day, which would you choose and what would be the most noticeable consequences?
- If you could instantly master any skill, what would it be?
- If you could have any fictional character over for dinner, who would you choose and what would you serve them?
- What's your favorite kind of weather, and why?

## Già vinti — da non far regredire (6)

- What's your favorite thing to do on a rainy day?
- What's your favorite season and why?
