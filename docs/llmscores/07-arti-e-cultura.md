## Muri — da vincere (16)

- Create a brief outline for a novel where the protagonist discovers a hidden library that contains books written by future authors.
- What method would enable a blind person to learn a musical instrument that typically relies on visual cues, and what principles would it be based on?
- Describe a method for embedding a hidden message in a piece of music that remains inaudible to casual listeners and can be retrieved only with a secret key.
- In a museum, a painting has been authenticated as a genuine Rembrandt using style analysis, but radiocarbon dating suggests the canvas dates from a later period. What investigative steps could reconcile these conflicting findings?
- The painting 'The Starry Night' depicts a swirling sky. Some art historians argue this reflects van Gogh's mental state, while others see it as influenced by contemporary astronomical observations. Evaluate these perspectives.
- Describe the taste of a color to someone who has never seen.
- A man builds a house with all four sides facing south. A bear walks past. What color is the bear?
- What's a good opening line for a mystery novel?
- A man builds a house with all four walls facing south. A bear walks past the house. What color is the bear?
- What are the three primary colors in traditional painting?
- What's the best movie you've watched recently, and what did you like most about it?
- What sound does a dog make?
- Name three fruits and describe the color of each one.
- Describe the color blue to someone who has never seen it.
- What color is a ripe banana?
- What color is the sky during the day?
